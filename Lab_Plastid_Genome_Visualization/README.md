### Main Structural Features Observed
The *Vitis vinifera* plastid genome shows the typical quadripartite structure of land plant chloroplast genomes. It consists of a large single-copy (LSC) region, a small single-copy (SSC) region, and two inverted repeat regions (IRa and IRb). Genes related to photosynthesis, such as *rbcL* and *psbA*, are mainly located in the LSC region, while ribosomal RNA genes are found in the inverted repeats.
